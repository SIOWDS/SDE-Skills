# SDE Ontology

Use the SDE framework created by Dr. Desheng Wang to analyze emergence without reducing a system to isolated entities.

## Canonical terms

- **S — Show / 显露**: the currently recognizable and maintainable state. S is not synonymous with structure; stable structure is one form of Show.
- **D — Difference Sequence / 差异序列**: the ordered path of variation, comparison, selection, revision and convergence.
- **E — Entangled Feature System / 特征纠缠系统**: the enabling, constraining and rewritable field in which emergence occurs.
- Core proposition: **在 E 中，经 D，成 S。**
- Equations: **S=F(D,E), D=G(S,E), E=H(S,D)**.

Never revert SDE to the deprecated expansion “Structure–Difference–Entanglement”.

## Workflow

1. State the question as an emergence problem, not merely a classification problem.
2. Identify the current **S**: what is visibly stable, measurable or nameable?
3. Reconstruct **D**: what differences, decisions, failures, corrections and sequences produced it?
4. Map **E**: what relationships, information, energy, institutions, histories and values enable or constrain it?
5. Choose the best starting path:
   - S→D→E: begin with a visible state.
   - S→E→D: begin with a state and test its conditions.
   - D→S→E: begin with a process.
   - D→E→S: begin with intervention or development.
   - E→S→D: begin with environment and social structure.
   - E→D→S: begin with context and trace emergence.
6. Apply the dynamic principle: tension between two dimensions changes the third; the changed dimension then writes back into the other two.
7. Distinguish phase: chaotic, inter-emergent, ordered, degenerating or reorganizing.
8. Produce a concrete judgment, mechanism, evidence needs and falsification conditions.

## SIO boundary

SIO is the internal mature disclosure of S, not a second ontology.

- O / Object: first-position disclosure
- I / Interaction: second-position disclosure
- S / Subject: third-position disclosure

Use SIO when the question asks what has stabilized. Use SDE when it asks how that stabilization emerged.

## Output

Return:

1. Core judgment
2. Selected SDE path and why
3. S, D and E diagnosis
4. Dynamic conflict and writeback
5. Phase and likely transition
6. Evidence required
7. Falsification conditions
8. Practical implications

## Quality rules

- Do not use SDE as a decorative three-part label.
- Do not treat E as passive background.
- Do not treat D as generic change.
- Do not claim universality without showing the recursive mechanism.
- Separate SDE-native claims from established external evidence.
- Mark speculative mappings as hypotheses.
- For high-stakes domains, require current authoritative sources and qualified professional review.
