# SDE Research Forge · 创新研究锻造

**Direct value:** turn a promising thought into a defensible innovation pathway—from explanatory fracture, through mechanism and evidence, to publishable academic value.

This skill helps an AI produce not only a new idea, but also the research path, validation method and field contribution required for that idea to become real innovation.

Turn a conceptual fracture into a publishable research programme.

## Operating principle

Use SDE internally to diagnose emergence, but write the final paper in the target discipline's language unless the paper is explicitly about SDE Ontology.

## Workflow

1. **Define the field and audience**
   - target discipline, journal family and article type;
   - empirical, theoretical, methodological or review contribution.

2. **Locate the fracture**
   - identify two or more explanations that cannot jointly account for the same phenomenon;
   - distinguish a literature gap from a genuine explanatory failure.

3. **Map the system**
   - current observable state;
   - sequence of mechanisms and decisions;
   - environmental, informational, energetic and institutional conditions.

4. **Generate the conceptual core**
   - write one sentence that existing concepts cannot express without loss;
   - test whether it is merely a renamed established construct;
   - define scope and boundary cases.

5. **Build the mechanism**
   - specify actors or units;
   - state causal or generative steps;
   - identify feedback and writeback;
   - explain expected variation across conditions.

6. **Design adjudication**
   - derive rival predictions;
   - state confirming and disconfirming observations;
   - choose data and methods capable of distinguishing alternatives.

7. **Translate into field-native language**
   - remove decorative SDE terminology;
   - connect each new term to the target literature;
   - retain SDE terms only when SDE itself is the research object.

8. **Stress-test**
   - strongest alternative explanation;
   - endogeneity and reverse causality;
   - measurement validity;
   - generalization boundary;
   - ethical and practical risk;
   - self-application test.

9. **Produce the manuscript blueprint**
   - title;
   - research question;
   - abstract logic;
   - contribution statement;
   - theory sections;
   - hypotheses or propositions;
   - method;
   - evidence plan;
   - limitations;
   - falsification conditions.

## Required output

1. Explanatory fracture
2. Central mechanism
3. New concept and reducibility test
4. Rival explanations
5. Testable predictions
6. Evidence design
7. Field-native contribution paragraph
8. Manuscript outline
9. Target journal profile
10. Failure conditions

## Quality gates

A research forge is incomplete if:

- the “gap” is only that nobody combined two keywords;
- the new concept can be replaced by an established term without explanatory loss;
- mechanism is replaced by metaphor;
- evidence cannot distinguish the proposal from rivals;
- citations are invented or unverifiable;
- the proposal cannot state what would prove it wrong;
- disciplinary translation has not been completed.
